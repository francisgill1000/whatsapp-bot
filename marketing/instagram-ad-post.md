## Three things to A/B test if the first ad underperforms

1. **Pain headline** — swap "STOP MISSING BOOKINGS" for "YOUR SALON DESERVES BETTER" or "HIRE A 24/7 RECEPTIONIST" and see which CTR wins.
2. **Visual concept** — try a calm-only "after" shot (single panel, no split) for ad-fatigued audiences.
3. **Offer wording** — "Free to start" vs "First month free" vs "First 10 bookings free" — different commitment psychology.

Test ONE variable at a time, not all three. Run each variant 3 days minimum.

---

## What to do if this post flops (cost per conversation > AED 50)

1. **Check the bot is replying instantly** — pm2 logs on the droplet. A 5-min delay in WhatsApp reply kills the funnel more than any ad tweak.
2. **Check the audience** — if frequency >4 in 3 days, the audience is too narrow. Broaden or test a new lookalike.
3. **Change the creative** before changing the audience — copy/visual fatigue is the #1 cause.
4. **Don't pause early.** Meta needs 5–7 days minimum to find your buyer. Pausing on day 2 = waste of learning data.
